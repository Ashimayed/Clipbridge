ClipBridge

Copy on your PC, paste on your Android phone – and back again. ClipBridge syncs your clipboard between Chrome and Android using a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive. No server, no accounts, no tracking.

Get it

  • Chrome extension: coming soon on the Chrome Web Store
  • Android app: download the latest APK from the GitHub Releases page

How it works

  1. Install both the extension and the Android app.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account on both.
  3. Copy text on one device – it shows up on the other.
