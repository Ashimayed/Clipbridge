Installing the Chrome extension

  1. 1

    Click Download above to get ClipBridge-Chrome-Extension.zip. It's not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so it installs manually — takes about a minute.

  2. 2

    Unzip it somewhere you'll keep it. Chrome loads the extension from this folder every time, so don't delete or move it afterward.

  3. 3

    In Chrome, open chrome://extensions (paste that into the address bar and press Enter).

  4. 4

    Turn on Developer mode, top right.

  5. 5

    Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped ClipBridge-Chrome-Extension folder.

  6. 6

    Pin ClipBridge with the puzzle-piece icon, click it, and sign in with Google.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the extension and the Android app.

  2. 2

    Sign in with the same Google account on both.

  3. 3

    Copy text on one device — it shows up on the other.
