ClipBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

ClipBridge is a free, open tool that syncs your clipboard between the ClipBridge Chrome extension and the ClipBridge Android app. This policy explains what data ClipBridge touches and what it does with it.

What ClipBridge accesses

  • Clipboard content you choose to sync – text you copy on one device so it can appear on your other device.
  • Google Drive app data folder – ClipBridge requests only the drive.appdata scope. This gives it access to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only ClipBridge can see. It cannot read, list, or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your Google account sign-in – used only to get permission to use that app data folder.

Where your data goes

Clipboard data is stored only in your own Google Drive app data folder and on your own devices. ClipBridge has no server. The developer never receives, sees, stores, or shares your clipboard content, your account details, or any other personal data.

What ClipBridge does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No use of your data to train AI or machine-learning models.

ClipBridge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can remove ClipBridge's access and data at any time:

  1. Go to Google Account → Third-party apps & services, select ClipBridge, and remove access.
  2. To delete the stored app data, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find ClipBridge, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  3. Uninstall the extension and the Android app.
